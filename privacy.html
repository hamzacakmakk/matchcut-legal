Privacy Policy

MatchCut — last updated: September 2026

The short version

There is no account and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number, we run no server of our own, and there is no analytics SDK, no advertising and no crash reporting in the app. Your photos and projects are stored only on your phone and are never uploaded. One third party appears below because one exists in the build: your app store's purchase validation.

1. What we collect

Nothing. The app has no registration, no login and no user profile. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos. We do not build a profile of you, we do not sell or share anything about you, and we could not identify you if we wanted to.

This is not a policy choice layered on top of a data pipeline. There is no pipeline: the app contains no analytics or advertising code and makes no network request of its own.

2. Your photos

Photos you capture or import stay in the app's private storage on your device. Cropping, alignment, layout and export are all performed locally on the phone. No photo is ever uploaded — we cannot see a single image, and neither can anyone else through us.

When you export a finished post, the image is saved to your own photo library or handed to your system share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice; once you send it somewhere, that destination's privacy policy applies rather than ours.

If you delete a project inside the app, or delete the app itself, the photos it stored go with it.

3. Camera, photo library and motion

The app asks for three permissions, each only when the matching feature is used:

  • Camera — to shoot the before and after photos. The camera feed is displayed and captured on the device; it is not recorded, streamed or transmitted.
  • Photo library — to import an existing photo you choose, and to save your finished post. The app reads only the file you pick; it does not scan or index your library.
  • Motion and orientation — the alignment guide reads the device's gyroscope and accelerometer so it can tell you when the phone is back at the original shooting angle. These readings are used live on screen and are never stored or sent.

The app does not record audio and does not request your location. Photos you import may carry their own EXIF metadata from your camera; the app does not read it, add to it, or send it anywhere.

4. Purchases (your app store and RevenueCat)

MatchCut Pro is optional. If you buy it, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple or Google and validated through RevenueCat, which tells the app whether your subscription is active.

RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier generated on your device, plus your purchase and subscription status and basic technical details of the transaction. It receives none of your photos or projects — those never leave the phone. We never see your card details: payment happens inside your store account and we have no access to it.

If you never buy anything, no purchase data is created at all.

5. Tracking and advertising

MatchCut shows no advertising, contains no advertising or attribution SDK, and does not use the advertising identifier. Nothing you do in the app is tracked across other apps or websites, which is why the app never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission.

6. Children

MatchCut is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — in fact we collect nothing that identifies any user, of any age.

7. Your rights

We hold no account and nothing that names you, so there is no profile for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf — there is nothing on our side to act on. You hold all of it: delete a project inside the app, or remove the app to erase everything it stored.

8. Changes

If this ever changes — if a future version adds accounts, sync or analytics — this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be visible here.

9. Contact

For any privacy question, use the support email listed on the app's App Store page, or see Support.
