MatchCut is a before-and-after photo tool for people whose work only looks like work when you can see both sides of it — hairdressers, detailers, cleaners, renovators and trainers. Shoot the before. Come back when the job is done, and the before photo floats over your camera until the phone is back at the same angle. Then pick a layout and post it.

Everything happens on your phone. There is no account, no cloud and no upload: your photos are never sent anywhere by the app.

MatchCut is an independent app. It is not affiliated with Apple, Google, or any social platform.
